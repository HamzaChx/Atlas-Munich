# Student Guide: Immigration & KVR for International Students in Munich

## 0. What is the KVR?

**KVR = Kreisverwaltungsreferat**\
This is the main city office that handles:

-   registering your address
-   residence permits (for non-EU students)
-   emergency cases (expiring permits, *Fiktionsbescheinigung*)

You will mostly deal with:

-   **Bürgerbüro** → address registration
-   **Ausländerbehörde / Service Centre for Immigration** → residence
    permits, extensions, *Fiktionsbescheinigung*, emergencies

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Registering Your Address ("Anmeldung")

### Do I really have to register?

**Yes.** You must register within **14 days** of moving in.

You also need this paper for:

-   university enrollment (sometimes)
-   opening a bank account
-   mobile phone / internet contracts
-   receiving your **Tax ID** (sent to your registered address)

### Where do I go?

At a **Bürgerbüro** (citizen's office).\
There are several in Munich (Pasing, Orleansplatz, Scheidplatz, etc.).

**Important:**

-   You always need an **appointment** → booked online.
-   No walk-ins.

### What do I need to bring?

1.  Passport (and visa, if applicable)
2.  **Wohnungsgeberbestätigung**
    -   Form your landlord/dorm signs to confirm you live there
    -   Without this, you cannot register
3.  (Optional) Rental contract
4.  Registration form (download or get at the office)

### What happens at the appointment?

1.  You give your documents.
2.  Staff enters your data.
3.  You receive a **Meldebescheinigung** (registration certificate).

**Keep this safe** --- you'll need it for banks, insurance, etc.

### After the appointment

-   Your **Tax ID** arrives by post in a few weeks.
-   Make sure your **name is on the mailbox**.

## 2. Residence Permit for Studies

### Step 1 -- Before coming to Germany

You normally:

-   apply for a student visa in your home country
-   enter Germany with that visa (usually 3 months)
-   convert it to a residence permit in Munich

### Step 2 -- After arrival

1.  Find accommodation
2.  Register your address
3.  Collect documents
4.  Apply for your residence permit before your visa/90 days expire

### Who is responsible?

**Ausländerbehörde / Service Centre for Immigration**\
KVR, Ruppertstraße 19, 80337 München

You **do not walk in**.

You:

-   apply online or by post
-   wait while KVR processes
-   may be invited for fingerprints/photo/pick-up

### Which documents do I need?

-   Application form
-   Passport + visa
-   Biometric photo
-   University admission or enrollment certificate
-   **Proof of health insurance**
-   **Proof of sufficient funds**, e.g.:
    -   blocked account
    -   scholarship
    -   *Verpflichtungserklärung*
    -   parents' guarantee + financial evidence
-   Proof of address registration

### How to apply

1.  Go to the Munich Immigration website
2.  Fill out online form
3.  Upload documents (PDF/JPG)
4.  Submit
5.  Save the confirmation PDF

Later:

-   KVR checks documents
-   May request additional documents
-   You get an appointment to pick up your eAT card

### Validity & Costs

-   Usually **2--3 years** (or based on study program length)
-   Fees:
    -   First permit: \~100 €
    -   Extension: \~93--96 €
    -   Some scholarships = no fee

Payment: **cash or EC card** (no credit cards).

### Can I work with a student residence permit?

Yes:

-   Typically **120 full days OR 240 half days** per year\
    (Updated rules may allow **140/280** --- check current info.)

More work requires special permission or another permit.

## 3. Extending Your Residence Permit

Your permit has an expiry date. Extend it **early**.

### When should I apply?

**3--4 months before expiry.**

### What do I need?

-   Passport
-   Current permit
-   Biometric photo
-   New enrollment certificate
-   Health insurance proof
-   Proof of funds
-   **Study progress proof** (after \~4th semester):
    -   transcript
    -   or certificate confirming active progress

If you changed universities/programs:

-   exmatriculation letter
-   new enrollment certificate

### What if my permit expires but I applied in time?

You get **Fiktionswirkung**:

-   Your old permit remains valid until a decision is made.
-   You may need a **Fiktionsbescheinigung** as proof.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Fiktionsbescheinigung -- Temporary Proof of Status

A **Fiktionsbescheinigung** is a temporary paper stating:

> Your previous residence permit stays valid while your new application
> is processed.

It:

-   allows legal stay
-   often allows travel
-   confirms work rights (if previous permit allowed work)

### When do I need it?

-   When your card expires before the new one is ready
-   When traveling soon
-   When an employer/authority demands updated proof

### How to get one

1.  Apply to extend your permit
2.  If your card is about to expire, contact the KVR
3.  For urgent cases → emergency appointment

You usually need:

-   passport
-   sometimes a biometric photo
-   proof of application

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Emergency Appointments at the KVR

Used when:

-   your permit is expiring / expired
-   urgent travel (7 days)
-   risk of losing a job or benefits

### What counts as an emergency?

Valid emergencies include:

-   risk of losing job
-   risk of losing benefits (BAföG, scholarship, unemployment aid)
-   urgent travel due to:
    -   serious illness of a close relative
    -   funeral
    -   unavoidable business trip

### How to book

-   Emergency slots appear online **same day** (early morning)
-   They disappear quickly
-   If impossible, call the service line

Do **not** show up without an appointment --- security will turn you
away.

### Bring to the emergency appointment

Proof of emergency:

-   Letter from employer / authorities
-   Travel proof + explanation
-   Medical/funeral documents

Plus:

-   passport
-   current residence card
-   application confirmation

Usually you get a **Fiktionsbescheinigung** on the same day.

## 6. Important Contacts & Tips

### Main Offices

**Immigration Office (Ausländerbehörde)**\
- Address: KVR, Ruppertstraße 19, 80337 Munich\
- Phone: +49 89 233-96010\
- Appointment required

**Bürgerbüros (Citizen's Offices)**\
- Multiple locations\
- Appointment required

### Practical student tips

-   Put your name on your mailbox.
-   Scan/photograph all documents (cloud + USB).
-   Apply months before your permit expires.
-   English is usually fine; universities can help translate.
-   **Never pay agents** for KVR appointments --- official bookings are
    free.
